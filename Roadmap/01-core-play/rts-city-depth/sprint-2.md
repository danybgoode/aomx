# RTS city depth: faction verbs, city events and a contested Cibeles · Sprint 2: The city fights back

**Status:** ⬜ Planned

## Stories

### S2.1 City events with counterplay

As a player, the city interrupts the war fairly. Marcha en Reforma, Lluvia de las 5, Obra eterna and Se fue la luz are announced 20 seconds ahead, apply to everyone, and can be toggled off in setup.

### S2.2 Cibeles control and plusvalía

As a player, holding Cibeles is a fight. Holding it gives vision at 30 seconds, extra Varos at 60 and the win at 120, and only fighters can hold. My buildings spread plusvalía that flips neutral blocks, and enemy buildings inside it lose pop cap. The AI contests both. Includes the public release.
