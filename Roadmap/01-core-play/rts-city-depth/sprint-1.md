# RTS city depth: faction verbs, city events and a contested Cibeles · Sprint 1: Verbs

**Status:** ⬜ Planned

## Stories

### S1.1 Faction verbs

As a player, each faction plays differently. Oaxaqueños have Tequio, where shared builds go superlinear. Veracruzanos have Bocinas, a placed area claim that spreads and buffs. Poblanos have Talavera walls and Mole, a long-cooking timed buff. Regios have dollarized Varos, an exchange rate that swings so they can bank and convert. The AI uses all four, and the scenario report keeps faction win rates in a band.

### S1.2 Archetype verbs

As a player, my units have tricks. The Godín's Junta stuns a group. The Asambleísta's march blocks a road for 20 seconds. The Mirrey's tarjetazo buys a neutral building but needs a valet parking spot. The Broker converts a building with a mixed-use render. The AI uses each one.
