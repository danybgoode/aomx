# A first match that works · Sprint 1: The first ten minutes

**Status:** ⬜ Planned

## Stories

### S1.1 Openings with decisions

As a newcomer, I start with three workers, a bicitaxi scout and a small stockpile, and every purchase costs something, so the match asks me to decide from second five. The base pop cap rises to 6 and unit and building costs go up until, by 2:00, a newcomer has spent at least 80% of their income. Done when the content version is bumped, saves and replays either migrate or are labelled incompatible, the host runs the same content, and the opponent scenario report shows the first fight before any hold victory on every difficulty.

### S1.2 Cibeles you can see coming

As a player, I can't lose to a center hold I never saw. Hold victory unlocks at minute 6 on the two lower difficulties (scaled down on higher ones), only fighters count as holding, and when a hold starts I get an alert, a minimap ping and a one-tap camera jump. The result screen names the cause and gives one tip.

### S1.3 A readable, clickable economy

As a player, I can tell resources apart and command them without fighting the interface. The four sources get distinct silhouettes (taco stand, hardware store, ATM, vacant lot). Hit areas get larger. Double-click selects every visible unit of that type. Build stays available with several workers selected. The build menu becomes art cards that pause solo play, and notices go in a top region that never covers the battlefield.

### S1.4 The city on screen

As a player, I see a city, not a diamond in a void. A collapsible HUD gives the battlefield at least 75% of the window at 1440×900 and 1280×720. The map edge fades into rooftops and a skyline, and the match opens on my base with Cibeles in frame. Includes the public release and two uncoached newcomer reads recorded in `docs/qa/`.
