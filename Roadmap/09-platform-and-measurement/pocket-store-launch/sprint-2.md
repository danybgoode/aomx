# La Roma Pocket in the stores, measured and fair · Sprint 2: Ship to the stores

**Status:** ⬜ Planned

## Stories

### S2.1 Native builds

As a phone player, I install Pocket from Play, then from the App Store once iOS signing exists (D9). It goes through internal testing first, then production.

### S2.2 Store pages and compliance

As a store visitor, I see ES and EN listings and screenshots. Privacy labels, the data-safety form and the ratings questionnaire are filed.

### S2.3 A measured launch

As the owner, public pocket builds send eligible events (D5), the funnel reconciles per mode, and the runbook covers the stores with live verification recorded.
