# La Roma Pocket in the stores, measured and fair · Sprint 1: Fair money

**Status:** ⬜ Planned

## Stories

### S1.1 Pass and cosmetics store

As a player, I can buy the premium Pase de quincena and cosmetics through App Store and Google Play billing, with server-side receipt verification and refunds handled. Web stays free unless the owner decides otherwise (D8).

### S1.2 Fairness and safety

As a player, money never buys power: a test fails if any purchasable item changes a stat. Spend notices, purchase history and an updated privacy notice ship with it.
