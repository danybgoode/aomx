# Hora Pico: defend your flat (roguelite tower defense) · Sprint 1: Defend your flat

**Status:** ⬜ Planned

## Stories

### S1.1 Streets as paths

As a player, I defend a real-feeling block. Three maps (Roma, Doctores, Narvarte) come from `content/geography`, with intersections as build slots, and wave and tower logic run in a deterministic simulation.

### S1.2 Defenders and waves

As a player, my neighbours are the defence. Taquero splash, Asambleísta march slow, Bicitaxi intercept, Godín ranged and Barista buff come from the canon. Waves of Airbnb hosts, valets, Mirreyes and brokers end in a boss, and rush-hour waves change the pace.

### S1.3 A card after every wave

As a player, I choose one of three cards after each wave (defender upgrades or city events) across a 10-wave run. The run earns meta rewards and emits funnel events.
