# La Roma en 30 Turnos: the micro turn-based 4X · Sprint 1: Turn-based CDMX

**Status:** ⬜ Planned

## Stories

### S1.1 A hex map of the city

As a player, I expand across a seeded hex map built from the geography data, with colonias as cities, terrain and the four resources.

### S1.2 Expand and out-plan

As a player, I grow cities and recruit archetypes. Ages advance through a tech tree with age-up cards from the canon, each city pays rent, and I win on score after 30 turns or by conquest.

### S1.3 AI rivals

As a solo player, I face three AI levels that use the factions' verbs.
