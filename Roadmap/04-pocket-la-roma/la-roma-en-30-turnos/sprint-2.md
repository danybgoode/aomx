# La Roma en 30 Turnos: the micro turn-based 4X · Sprint 2: Play with friends

**Status:** ⬜ Planned

## Stories

### S2.1 Pass-and-play

As a group, two to four of us can share one phone.

### S2.2 Async online

As friends, we play async games through room codes on the match host. Each turn is submitted and validated by the server, and nudges are in-app.
