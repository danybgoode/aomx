# La Mudanza: the packing puzzle with a renovation meta · Sprint 1: Pack and renovate

**Status:** ⬜ Planned

## Stories

### S1.1 The packing puzzle

As a player, I fit moving-box pieces into the truck and room grid, clearing rows and columns, with special items like the garrafón, the speaker and the plant, each with its own rule.

### S1.2 Level generator and validator

As the owner, levels come from a seeded generator with a difficulty curve and a solvability check, with 60 tuned levels at launch.

### S1.3 The renovation meta

As a player, stars renovate my block, and the satire turns on me: landlord notes, rising rent, neighbours moving out.
