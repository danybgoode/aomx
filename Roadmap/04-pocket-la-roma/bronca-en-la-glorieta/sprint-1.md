# Bronca en la Glorieta: the real-time lane duel · Sprint 1: A duel against the machine

**Status:** ⬜ Planned

## Stories

### S1.1 The lane simulation

As a player, I fight across two lanes, each side with two posts and a Cibeles. Matches last 3 minutes plus 1 of overtime, in a deterministic lockstep-ready simulation.

### S1.2 Cards and Varos

As a player, I build an eight-card deck from canon archetypes plus two event cards (Marcha, Quincena), Varos regenerate over time, and I drag a card into a lane.

### S1.3 Bot opponents

As a player, I can practise offline against three bot levels and earn offline trophies.
