# Pleito de Vecindad: the auto-battler · Sprint 2: Real neighbours

**Status:** ⬜ Planned

## Stories

### S2.1 Live ghosts

As a player, I fight boards other people actually built. Each round's board uploads once it's verified. Later runs face ghosts matched by round and strength, falling back to preset rivals. Ghosts show their colonia and a generated alias.

### S2.2 Chamba del día

As a player, I come back for today's fixed seed, where everyone gets the same shop rolls and events, and I see my colonia's leaderboard. Scores come from verified runs only.

### S2.3 Balance and share

As the owner, I see that no trait dominates: a bot balance report over 1,000 simulated runs keeps each trait's win rate inside an agreed band. As a player, I can replay a run and share it as a link, with a GIF clip on Web.
