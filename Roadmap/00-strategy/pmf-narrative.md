# PMF Narrative — Battle for La Roma

## Initial insight

Battle for La Roma applies the resource gathering, settlement building, age advancement, and warfare of classic real-time strategy to a satirical Mexico City whose factions draw on Mexican regional identities. (your words)
Its cultural specificity may make a familiar strategy loop feel fresh while remaining readable to players who miss the references. (assumed)

## Problem to solve

**Outcome:** Play a strategically deep RTS with a distinctive setting and factions. (your words)
**Motivation:** Enjoy classic RTS decisions alongside Mexican humor, regional rivalries, and contemporary urban satire. (your words)
**Gaps:**
- Players seeking this combination may have few games that offer both a familiar RTS loop and this particular setting. (assumed)
- Cultural jokes could obscure objectives for players unfamiliar with Mexico unless the core game communicates them clearly. (your words)

The player should be able to understand the economy, combat, and path to La Roma before recognizing any cultural reference. (your words)

## Target audience

**Now:** Players familiar with Age of Empires II and classic RTS games, especially Mexican players who enjoy regional humor and cultural references. (your words)
**Future:** Strategy enthusiasts seeking unusual factions, then international players, casual players drawn by the visual identity, and content creators. (your words)

The first audience can judge whether the strategy works; later audiences test whether the game reads clearly beyond its references. (assumed)
