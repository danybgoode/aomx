# Battle for La Roma — Horizon

These destinations describe player and product end states. An epic names the destination it advances in its `destination:` frontmatter field. They do not imply that a game release or measured cohort already exists.

## complete-solo-battle — A complete solo battle
A classic RTS player can gather, build, command units and reach a clear win, loss or draw against an authored opponent.

## trusted-launch-measurement — A trustworthy launch cohort
An invited player's first open, match start, accepted result and later-day return reconcile from offline play to auditable Golden Frijoles readings.

## fight-for-la-roma — The Battle for La Roma promise
Regional factions can contest Mexico City neighborhoods for La Roma through readable strategy, with cultural humor that does not hide the rules.
