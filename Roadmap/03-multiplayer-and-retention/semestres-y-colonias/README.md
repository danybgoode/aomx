# Semestres y colonias: seasons, clans and sharing

## Why

The top-grossing games run on seasons, and creators run on clips. A Semestre brings a new district, two archetypes and a free Pase de quincena. Colonias are clans with no free text, competing in a weekend fight for Cibeles across every mode. Replays from the RTS, Pleito and Bronca become shareable links.
