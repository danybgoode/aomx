## Stories

### S2.1 Colonias

As a player, I can create or join a colonia with a preset name and emblem (no free text, so no moderation; D4), see its members and its leaderboard.

### S2.2 The weekend at Cibeles

As a colonia, we earn points from any mode in a weekend contest for Cibeles, with live standings.

### S2.3 Clips and share links

As a player, I share RTS, Pleito and Bronca replays as links that open in a web replay viewer, with a GIF clip on Web and share cards on the landing and itch pages.
