# Del cuarto de azotea al rooftop: the meta and the idle layer · Sprint 1: The apartment

**Status:** ⬜ Planned

## Stories

### S1.1 Four apartment stages

As a player, every finished match in any mode earns city experience, and my apartment moves through four stages with art and a running rent gag. Each stage unlocks pocket archetypes according to the canon's unlock age, never a stat boost.

### S1.2 Cosmetics and the collection

As a player, I earn board skins, token frames and apartment decor by playing, and I see them in a collection screen. Nothing is sold in this epic.

### S1.3 The quincena chest and prestige

As a regular, a weekly quincena chest gives cosmetics and decor. After stage IV I can reset to the rooftop room with a prestige badge.
