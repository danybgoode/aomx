# Idea — Prepare a release candidate

After a finishable, distinctive and measurable match exists, refine the distribution build, settings, localization, accessibility, performance, crash reporting, support and community loop into reviewable work. Set a release decision from observed play and quality evidence. The provisional 15% later-day completion goal has no baseline until the first eligible cohort matures; a merge to `master` is not a deployment.
