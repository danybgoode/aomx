# Idea — Build the audio and effects identity

The source taxonomy includes audio, although there is no audio concept board. `effects.jpg` suggests animation and VFX but is a flattened illustration. Refine a small original event-to-presentation pack for order confirmation, gather/deliver, construction, combat, hit/destruction, objective contest/capture and terminal outcome. Preserve non-audio cues for every important state; effects may never obscure units, selection, fog or commands. Add animation timing, frame budgets and accessible reduced-effects settings to the asset contract. Reuse the existing order-confirm sound and original faction audio where they pass style and rights review.

A later wave can add neighborhood ambience, music, Mexico City traffic/market texture and original Spanish voice barks with mix buses, subtitles/captions, license provenance and mobile/Web autoplay/performance checks. Weather, day/night, vehicles, culture and other illustrated systems remain candidate mechanics requiring separate approval. See the [coverage review](../art-world-coverage-2026-10-21.md).
