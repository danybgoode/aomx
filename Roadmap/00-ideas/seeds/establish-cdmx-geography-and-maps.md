# Idea — Establish CDMX geography and map data

Refine a data and playability foundation for the 16 CDMX alcaldías, featured colonias, real public places, and the fictionalized playable territory layer. Acquire and review authoritative geography and use terms/administrative placements correctly; record source dates, geometry transforms and redistribution licenses **before importing data**. The illustrated `full-map.jpg` is art direction, not GIS or an approved conquest rule. Keep region/faction selection independent of map location: any of the four regional factions can fight on any fair map.

The first vertical slice should specify stable territory/place IDs, a verified Roma Norte/Cibeles map, one selectable mission or skirmish card, and map metadata for traversable/blocked/decorative, spawn fairness, objective state, save/replay and localization. Do not replace the current functional maps until the new map passes pathing, AI, fog, selection and both victory-route checks. Catalog the remaining landmarks and uncertain placements for later scenarios; use the same map contract for campaign and standalone skirmish. Scope the GIS source review as high risk because licenses and geographic assertions can propagate to commercial content.
