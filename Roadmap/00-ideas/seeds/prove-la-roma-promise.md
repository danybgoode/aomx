# Idea — Prove the La Roma promise (E3 candidate)

**Why it is in the backlog:** The design prework describes regional factions, contemporary Mexico City satire and a fight for La Roma. The first measured playtest needs to test those distinctive choices, not only generic RTS controls. Shape E3 after E2 produces a finishable solo match.

## Candidate outcome

Two faction prototypes lead to meaningfully different, counterable strategies. A contested district changes where and when a player fights. Both culturally familiar and unfamiliar RTS testers can explain the objective and their next choice without coaching; humor enriches play without being needed to understand it.

## Candidate iterations

- **C1a:** Add and test two faction prototypes with visible strategic differences and counters.
- **C1b:** Add a contested district, clear objective feedback and short onboarding.

## Decisions to make when refining

- Choose faction mechanics from [the design prework](../../../docs/design-prework/civilizations/04-factions.md) after checking balance, comprehension and cultural portrayal.
- Define what contesting or winning a district changes in the match without overloading the first skirmish.
- Set a small playtest sample and comprehension checks for players with and without the cultural context.
- Coordinate with the [approved launch funnel](../../09-platform-and-measurement/track-launch-play-funnel/README.md) so invited testers have complete, accepted funnel data.

This idea stays in the [generated build order](../BUILD-ORDER.md) as a raw seed until E2's finishable match informs its scope and the owner approves a specific bet.
