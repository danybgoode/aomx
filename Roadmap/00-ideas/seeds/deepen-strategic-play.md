# Idea — Deepen strategic play

The [design prework](../../../docs/design-prework/design/00-master-gdd.md) proposes four resources, four ages, research, fog, counters, siege and defenses. Keep these as the full-game ambition. After the first finishable match and playtests, use `refine` to choose the smallest addition that changes an actual player decision and has a counter. Do not treat proposed names or balance numbers as approved.
