# The La Roma canon: archetypes, traits, events and voice · Sprint 2: Archetype art

**Status:** ⬜ Planned

## Stories

### S2.1 Battlefield sprites

As an RTS player, I recognize each archetype by silhouette: 12 archetypes as team-coloured pixel sprites at the current unit scale, with idle, walk and attack frames to the sprite animation spec, made through the owned art pipeline with manifest and licence trace. Done when they're distinguishable in a grayscale lineup and load in the RTS art catalog.

### S2.2 Cards, portraits and icons

As a pocket player, I see the same cast as cards and portraits: 12 card portraits, 9 trait icons, an icon per event, and four apartment-stage illustrations (rooftop room to rooftop bar). They go in the asset manifest with a visual QA sheet.
