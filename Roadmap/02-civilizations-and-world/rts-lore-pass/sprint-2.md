# RTS lore pass: archetypes, the city economy and the four-act campaign · Sprint 2: The city economy

**Status:** ⬜ Planned

## Stories

### S2.1 Renta

As a player, gentrification squeezes me. Each building has upkeep that rises with every age, and Terrenos reduce it. The HUD shows what's due, and the AI budgets for it.

### S2.2 The quincena and rush hour

As a player, time of day matters. From Edad Godín, income pulses every 90 seconds. Avenues slow to half speed during a visible rush-hour timer, and Metro stations let a garrison travel between them.

### S2.3 Age-up choice cards

As a player, each age-up is a choice between two cards, for example Fotocopias (techs −30%) or Aguas locas (+speed, −accuracy). Each card has a measurable effect, and the AI picks too.
