# RTS lore pass: archetypes, the city economy and the four-act campaign · Sprint 1: Archetypes on the battlefield

**Status:** ⬜ Planned

## Stories

### S1.1 Units become archetypes

As a player, I command people, not unit classes: worker → Foráneo, scout/raider → Bicitaxista, light melee → Asambleísta, ranged → Godín, heavy → Mirrey, siege → Broker, and a new support → Barista. Names, sprites and plain rule text come from the canon, and buildings are renamed from it too.

### S1.2 Barks and faction cards

As a player, units talk back when I select or order them or use an ability. The faction picker becomes four cards with art, one verb and a plain rule each, instead of a stat line.

### S1.3 Raider and support roles

As a player, I have new jobs to do. The Bicitaxista steals cargo from workers and will ignore traffic once it exists, and the Barista heals. The AI trains and uses both.
