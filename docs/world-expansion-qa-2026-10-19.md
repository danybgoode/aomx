## Authored maps and openings

La Roma retains its existing geometry. Narvarte has separated street blocks and open crossings. Copilco has two long barriers with multiple openings, elevated ground and cliffs. Both new definitions pin all four resource types, starts, command posts, construction anchors, district and AI target. Visual roads, labels and props do not change collision.

Routes are shortest traversable paths from each starting worker to its own source; the center figure uses any tile in the 4×4 hold zone. `world_expansion_test.gd` enforces opening route parity within one tile, reachable resources, 36 faction/map/AI-level starts, save/replay and both terminal rules. Its terminal checks set up the final state deliberately; they prove rule availability and precedence, not a human full match or natural AI victory. The 12 standard-level 700-tick fixtures pin deterministic hashes and replay.
