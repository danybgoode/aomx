## Versioned playable rules

| Choice | Exact version-5 rule | Cost and usable counter |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Regios | Carry 5; fighter Varos cost +1; standard movement, gathering and building HP. | Opponents can pressure before the extra carry pays back its fighter surcharge. |
| Poblanos | Buildings have 120% HP; carry 4; standard movement and gathering. | Siege still damages structures; their workers have no speed bonus. |
| Oaxaqueños | Gather every 2 ticks rather than 3; carry 4; buildings have 90% HP. Universitaria `cooperative_build_i` raises construction/repair rate by 1 for 4 Comida, 4 Materiales, 3 Varos, 1 Terrenos. | Focus their lighter post or contest their exposed workers before support finishes. |
| Veracruzanos | Move every 2 ticks rather than 3; carry 4; buildings have 85% HP; fighters cost +1 Varos. Universitaria `street_exchange_i` adds 1 Varos to a non-Varos delivery for 4 Comida, 3 Materiales, 4 Varos, 1 Terrenos. | Hold a chokepoint or attack their lighter post; the fighter surcharge limits an early rush. |

These integers are the builder's testable balance proposal under the approved fictional directions. No uncoached balance or portrayal verdict has been collected. The existing four-resource, four-age, two-victory simulation remains canonical.

## Authored maps and openings

La Roma retains its existing geometry. Narvarte has separated street blocks and open crossings. Copilco has two long barriers with multiple openings, elevated ground and cliffs. Both new definitions pin all four resource types, starts, command posts, construction anchors, district and AI target. Visual roads, labels and props do not change collision.

Routes are shortest traversable paths from each starting worker to its own source; the center figure uses any tile in the 4×4 hold zone. `world_expansion_test.gd` enforces opening route parity within one tile, reachable resources, 36 faction/map/AI-level starts, save/replay and both terminal rules. Its terminal checks set up the final state deliberately; they prove rule availability and precedence, not a human full match or natural AI victory. The 12 standard-level 700-tick fixtures pin deterministic hashes and replay.
