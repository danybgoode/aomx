## Gameplay gaps and next rules slice

The `feat/renewable-resource-play` follow-up now adds a worker **Plant Field** command. A legal 1×1 urban-garden plot costs 4 Materiales and 2 Varos, takes 24 worker ticks to mature, yields 80 Comida, supports adjacent gatherers, and can be reseeded after depletion. An AI worker creates or replants a field when its authored food sites run out. Fields have their own illustrated site sprite, are included in deterministic snapshots and saves, and raise the game/state versions to `godot-4.7.2-skirmish-9` / schema 6. Focused field, client, art and hash checks passed locally; this branch has no tester upload or complete balance result yet.

The `feat/explorable-map-sizes` follow-up adds a 56×56 La Roma Medium map, keeps the 28×28 small boards and 96×96 Large board, and labels all three sizes in match setup. Medium and Large each have four starter and four expansion sites per side plus eight neutral sites that either side can discover and gather. The neutral sites move by a recorded match seed, so a new solo match has a fresh but replayable layout. The minimap remembers explored sites. Small boards retain their authored compact starter layouts. The exact stuck match cannot be replayed because no save is available.
