## Install the current build

1. Move the old `Battle for La Roma Alpha.app` that macOS called damaged to the Bin. That build had an invalid code signature. Download `preview-dd25696f2712` from itch.io once the owner-only upload is authorized; confirm the downloaded ZIP checksum matches the value above. Safari may expand the ZIP into an app automatically. macOS may name the current copy `Battle for La Roma Alpha-2.app` or another numbered variant when a previous copy is present.
2. Verify the newly downloaded app before opening it:

   ```sh
   codesign --verify --deep --strict --verbose=2 "$HOME/Downloads/Battle for La Roma Alpha-2.app"
   ```

   Safari may add a different number to the app name if an older copy remains in Downloads; use the exact name shown there. It should say `valid on disk` and `satisfies its Designated Requirement`. If it reports an invalid signature or macOS still says the app is damaged, stop and report the version shown on itch.io. Do not try to open the old build by removing its quarantine attribute.
3. Move the new app into `/Applications`, then open it. This private preview has a valid ad hoc signature but no Apple notarization. If macOS says its developer cannot be verified, use **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway**, then confirm **Open**. This makes an exception for this app; there is no need to disable Gatekeeper globally. [Apple's instructions](https://support.apple.com/en-ie/102445) and [Godot's macOS guide](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/4.7/tutorials/export/running_on_macos.html) describe this path.

## Play and check the outbox

1. Check that the window shows `Battle for La Roma Alpha`, an invite-code field, sync status and a setup choice between Regios and Poblanos. Before choosing, confirm it states both win routes: destroy the opposing command post or hold La Roma for 60 seconds. Record a screenshot.
2. Check the board and setup text at normal window size. Choose Poblanos; the HUD should show `preview-dd25696f2712`, `Poblanos vs Regios`, the marked 3×3 district and a neutral hold state. Press **+** and **-** or scroll to zoom the board, **0** to reset, and **G** to toggle fullscreen. Select a blue unit at each zoom level; its highlight should stay on the tile you clicked. The invite field, HUD and bottom controls should remain readable.
3. Turn Wi-Fi off and play the Poblanos match. Your stockpile starts at **0**. Left-click the small blue worker near your HQ, then right-click the nearby gold circle. A short marker should say **Gathering gold**, and the HUD should repeat it under **Last action**. Poblanos workers deliver four gold per trip. Watch **Stockpile** in the HUD, rather than **Gold left** at the source. After two trips, Stockpile should reach at least **6**.
4. With the worker selected and at least 6 in your stockpile, press **B** and point at a clear 2×2 patch near your HQ outside the district. A yellow-green preview means you can build; red means the footprint is blocked. Left-click a valid patch and wait for the worker to finish the recruitment **POST**. Try one blocked patch as well: the board marker should turn red and the HUD should explain the rejection. Press **Esc** to cancel placement. Click your blue HQ and press **1** to queue another worker (cost 4). Click the completed POST and press **2** for melee (cost 3) or **3** for ranged (cost 4). Poblanos' command and recruitment posts have 20% more health. Each successful order should show a short confirmation.
5. Move a blue fighter into the marked center. The HUD should show the blue side holding La Roma and increasing progress. Move the fighter out or let a red fighter contest it; progress should reset. Win either by holding uncontested for 60 seconds or by destroying the red command post. If yours falls, you lose; an unresolved match draws after 15 minutes. The computer should contest the center and pressure a command post. Record the route and result.
6. Press **R** to return to faction choice and choose Regios. Confirm workers deliver five gold per trip and melee/ranged cost one more gold than Poblanos (4 and 5). Finish a Regios match by either route, checking the economy and district state along the way. Record any unclear rule text.
7. At the result, verify the HUD says the event is saved and pending sync. Quit the app, reopen it, and verify the saved state remains. Press **R** to choose a new match. Record the result and restart screens.
8. Once a fresh **internal QA invite code** is issued and the reviewed ingest version is deployed, enter it, turn Wi-Fi back on and press **F** to retry sync. Expect a synced **test** receipt; retry with **F** and confirm the receipt does not change. The operator checks that no public cohort event or numeric reading was created, including for a preview client that claims `test_account: false`. Without an invite code, pending sync is expected. The earlier `owner_qa_20261014` code was used and revoked on 2026-10-15; it cannot be reused for the new preview.

Before external invitations, verify that an anonymous visit cannot see the Restricted page and that a temporary download key works outside the creator session. Revoke the test key afterward. A Developer ID signature and notarization would remove the manual Gatekeeper step, but require Apple developer credentials that are not configured for this preview.
