## Install the current build

1. Download `preview-27661d2a0900` from itch.io after Butler marks it ready. Safari may expand the download into an app automatically. The checksum above identifies the locally uploaded source ZIP; itch.io may package the browser download differently. macOS may add a number to the app name when a previous copy is present.
2. Verify the newly downloaded app before opening it:

   ```sh
   codesign --verify --deep --strict --verbose=2 "$HOME/Downloads/Battle for La Roma Alpha.app"
   ```

   Use the exact app name shown in Downloads. It should say `valid on disk` and `satisfies its Designated Requirement`. If it reports an invalid signature or macOS says the app is damaged, stop and report the version shown on itch.io.
3. Move the new app into `/Applications`, then open it. This private preview has a valid ad hoc signature but no Apple notarization. If macOS says its developer cannot be verified, use **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway**, then confirm **Open**. This makes an exception for this app; there is no need to disable Gatekeeper globally. [Apple's instructions](https://support.apple.com/en-ie/102445) and [Godot's macOS guide](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/4.7/tutorials/export/running_on_macos.html) describe this path.

## Play and check the outbox

1. Check that the window shows `Battle for La Roma Alpha` and a setup choice between Regios and Poblanos. Before choosing, confirm it states both win routes: destroy the opposing command post or hold La Roma for 60 seconds. The invite field and sync diagnostics are in the **...** menu after choosing. Record a screenshot.
2. Check the board and setup text at normal window size. Choose Poblanos; the top bar should show **Poblanos** and **0 gold**, with a blue headquarters against a red one, a central plaza objective and a neutral hold state in the collapsible objectives widget. Open **...** and confirm the build ID named above. Check that terrain, carts, jacarandas, buildings and units have transparent edges and that friend/enemy colors remain clear. Resize the window larger and smaller: the battlefield and top/bottom HUD should scale together. Press **+** and **-** or scroll to adjust board zoom, **0** to fit the current window, and **G** to toggle fullscreen. Select a blue unit at its visible body at each zoom level; its highlight should stay on that actor. The top bar, minimap and bottom command bar should remain readable. Press **Reset**, then Cancel to keep progress; press it again and confirm to begin a fresh Poblanos match. **R** should still return to faction choice.
3. Turn Wi-Fi off and play the Poblanos match. Your stockpile starts at **0**. Left-click the blue worker's body near your HQ, then right-click the nearby gold cart. A short marker should say **Gathering gold** and a temporary notification should confirm the order. Poblanos workers deliver four gold per trip. Watch the top gold counter; the cart's **Gold** label is the source amount. After two trips, the stockpile should reach at least **6**.
4. With the worker selected and at least 6 in your stockpile, press **B** or the Build icon/card and point at a clear 2×2 patch near your HQ outside the district. A yellow-green preview means you can build; red means the footprint is blocked. Left-click a valid patch and wait for the worker to finish the recruitment **POST**. Try one blocked patch as well: the board marker should turn red and a notification should explain the rejection. Press **Esc** to cancel placement. Select the blue HQ and press **1** or its recruit card to queue another worker (cost 4). Select the completed POST and press **2** for melee (cost 3) or **3** for ranged (cost 4). The production queue and gold counter should update. Poblanos' command and recruitment posts have 20% more health.
5. Move a blue fighter into the marked center. Expand **Objectives** and confirm that its progress bar increases while the blue side holds La Roma. Move the fighter out or let a red fighter contest it; progress should reset. Win either by holding uncontested for 60 seconds or by destroying the red command post. If yours falls, you lose; an unresolved match draws after 15 minutes. The computer should contest the center and pressure a command post. Record the route and result.
6. Press **R** to return to faction choice and choose Regios. Confirm workers deliver five gold per trip and melee/ranged cost one more gold than Poblanos (4 and 5). Finish a Regios match by either route, checking the economy and district state along the way. Record any unclear rule text.
7. At the result, open **...** and verify the event is saved and pending sync. Quit the app, reopen it, and verify the saved state remains. Press **R** to choose a new match. Record the result and restart screens.
8. Once a fresh **internal QA invite code** is issued and the reviewed ingest version is deployed, enter it in **...**, turn Wi-Fi back on and press **F** to retry sync. Expect a synced **test** receipt in diagnostics; retry with **F** and confirm the receipt does not change.
